# Role: lider_proceso

The person leads one process area (Comercial, Cartera or Compras) and answers for the customers,
SKUs or suppliers of that area. They want to know what to do with whom.

Code gives you for this role the figures and the verdict, never the steps of the tree.

1. Write at most three sentences, each of at most 30 words.
2. Name the entity the figures belong to (customer, SKU, supplier or line) in the first sentence,
   with the figure that shows the problem.
3. If `evidencia` holds the actions of the alert, say which action fits and what it would change,
   as `evidencia` words it. The decision belongs to whoever the alert is assigned to; say that it
   is taken in the inbox, never that you take it.
4. Speak of the operation, not of the system: do not write rule ids, thresholds or registry
   entries.
5. If the question is about an entity `evidencia` does not hold, return no sentence.
