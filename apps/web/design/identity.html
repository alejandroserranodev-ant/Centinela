Centinela identity

The appearance of Centinela, written down before any screen is built. Each section answers a question Arena's style kernel asks. Once this page is approved, arena.config.json and the style plugin under design/centinela/ restate it in the form a build reads.

Until arena-to-prod has run in apps/web, this page reads Arena's reference sheet: the palette and the faces below are Centinela's, while the radius ladder is still drawn from Arena's reference plugin.

The product

A decision inbox, not a dashboard. The manager and the process leads of Distribuidora Andina S.A.S. open it and find each problem already explained, ordered by pesos at risk, with a proposal ready to approve, edit or reject. It is an application somebody works in: a list of alerts set against a reading column, a pinned chat, an audit log and a settings page. Every piece is furniture Arena ships. The two pieces drawn by hand are the alert row and the chat message.

The register starts from Arena's measured inbox entry, because the screen is a list read item by item against a reading column. Centinela departs from that entry in one place, density, which the air section below states.

The palette

Centinela's own brand. Light leads, because it is an office tool managers open during the working day. Petrol blue is the voice and carries the one primary action on a screen, "Aprobar". Amber is the distinction and the focus ring, never a second primary. The dark palette is the same hues, re-stepped for a dark surface.

Measured, not eyeballed. Primary on white is 7.8:1, amber on base-200 clears 4.5:1, and in dark mode every text colour clears 6.8:1. The chart ramp passes the colorblind checks of the dataviz validator in both modes. In light mode, three slots sit under 3:1 against white, so every Centinela chart carries direct labels and a table view. The "how I got here" view is that table. Status colours mean severity and are never series colours, and severity always carries a label and an icon besides its colour.

The type

Inter for headings and body, JetBrains Mono for figures, labels and SQL, all three served by Google Fonts. The mono face carries every amount in pesos, so that columns of money line up, and the queries behind "how I got here".

The character

Drawn. A hairline on every surface and between rows, corners almost square, and no shadow except on the surfaces that float: a sheet, a dialog, the command palette. It reads as a precise, auditable tool. A row is a line of text, not an object to pick up.

The air

Medium density in a wide column. This is the one departure from the inbox entry, which declares .arena-compact on the document. Centinela shows a handful of costly decisions rather than hundreds of messages, so each row gets room for its amount, its severity and its confidence. It is also read on a phone.

The media

Centinela shows no photographs. The only pictures are charts, which take their own box. The media roles keep the inbox answer, landscape three by two and filled, and nothing on screen exercises them.

The page

On a desktop, three zones fill the window. The navigation rail sits on the left (Bandeja, Bitácora, Configuración), then the list of alerts ordered by pesos at risk, then the reading column with the open alert, which stops at the prose measure. The chat opens as a side sheet over the reading column. The app bar carries the simulated day and the "Avanzar un día" control. On a phone the rail becomes a bottom bar, and the list and the alert stack as two routes. The air between components is the group step inside a row and the component step between blocks.

What this page does not settle

The copy and the brand mark. Nobody outside the product has to find it: it lives behind a login, so it carries no metadata beyond a page title. That is a decision, and it is why apps/web is a single-page application.
